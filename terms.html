1. The service

Cited is an informational research service. We ask public AI answer engines (currently Google Gemini; others as added) buyer-intent questions that name the business you submit, and we show you what those engines answered — including the competitors they recommended — with the engine's own words as evidence.

Cited does not authenticate users and does not verify that you own, work for, or are authorized to act for the business named in a scan. Anyone may run a scan on any business. You are responsible for how you use the results, and you may not present a report as the named business's own audit or speak on the business's behalf.

3. Public data only

We never access a scanned business's systems, website internals, or accounts. We only ask public AI engines questions and read their public answers. Nothing is installed, no credentials are collected, no infrastructure is probed.

4. Reports are public and unauthenticated

Every scan produces a shareable link that anyone can open without an account. Reports quote what public AI engines answered, including competitors they recommended; that is a record of engine output at scan time, not a statement by Cited about the scanned business. Share links only with people you intend to have them.

5. No guarantee of results — informational only

AI engine answers change without notice, vary between runs, and may name different competitors an hour later. A "RECOMMENDED" or "NOT FOUND" verdict reflects what the engine answered during the scan only. It is not an SEO audit, not a ranking guarantee, and not a representation about the business. Paid weekly monitoring runs on a schedule (currently weekly), not continuously.

6. The fix is a suggestion

The paid "specific fix" is informational guidance, not a service engagement. We do not edit your site, listings, or profiles.
